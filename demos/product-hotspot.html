Integrated visibility from every angle

The rear light is built directly into the fender, keeping it protected while preserving the bike's uninterrupted silhouette.

A wide LED surface creates a clear, consistent signal to traffic behind you, powered directly by the bike so there is nothing extra to charge or attach.

Quiet power, built into every rotation

The carbon belt drive replaces the traditional chain with a clean, low-maintenance system designed for everyday riding. No grease, no exposed drivetrain and almost no noise.

Paired with the integrated rear hub, power delivery feels smooth and immediate while keeping the silhouette of the bike remarkably simple.

Comfort shaped around the everyday ride

A carefully contoured saddle provides support without adding unnecessary bulk. Its soft-touch surface and subtle padding balance comfort with a slim, refined profile.

The integrated seatpost connection continues the frame's flowing geometry, creating a seamless transition from frame to rider.

Power that feels natural, not electric

The compact mid-drive system delivers assistance directly through the drivetrain for smooth acceleration and responsive handling.

Its low, central position keeps weight where it belongs, helping the bike feel balanced and predictable whether assistance is active or not.

Everything you need, right where you need it

The integrated cockpit brings speed, battery level and ride controls into a single display positioned naturally within your line of sight.

Controls remain close to your hands, while cables are carefully routed through the frame to keep the cockpit clean and distraction-free.
